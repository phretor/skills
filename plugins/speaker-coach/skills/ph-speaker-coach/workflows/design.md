# Workflow: `design` — Forward Design Interview

Use when the speaker has an idea but no outline yet. The shape is a strict, sequential Socratic interview. **One question per turn.** No multi-part bundles. No skipping ahead.

## Hard gates (cannot proceed without)

1. Venue type identified.
2. Target audience subset named (not "everyone").
3. 280-character takeaway written and approved.
4. 3+ supporting topic sentences written.
5. Per-sub-message narrative arcs sketched.

If the speaker tries to skip ahead (e.g., "let's just talk slides"), refuse and name the gate.

## Step-by-step script

### Step 1 — Universal prelude (covered in SKILL.md; do it before this workflow)

Confirm: venue, talk slug, artifact file path.

### Step 2 — Speaker archetype lens (optional but recommended)

Run the 4-question self-assessment from `references/archetypes.md`. Identify the dominant archetype + adjacent growth edge. Save in the artifact under "Archetype." Use it only to tune *how* you push, not *what* you push.

### Step 3 — Target audience subset

Ask: "Who specifically do you want this talk to serve? Not everyone in the room — the subset."

Options to surface if speaker is vague:

- Prospect customers — what trust signals will they read?
- Press — what's the "so what" and concrete example?
- Peers — what are the weak spots they'll prosecute?
- Decision-makers / executives — what action do you want them to take Monday?
- Policy / regulators — what's the systemic implication?

Persist the choice + the *signal* this subset reads for (from `design-principles.md`).

### Step 4 — The 280-character takeaway (hard gate)

> *Quote the speaker the rule:* "You have 280 characters. One sentence, content-rich, self-contained. If someone in the audience posts a tweet about your talk, what would you want that tweet to be?"

Loop:

1. Speaker proposes the takeaway.
2. Coach checks against the three-test bar (`design-principles.md` §2): **actionable, memorable, true**.
3. If any test fails, give *specific* feedback, cite which test, and ask for a revision.
4. Continue until all three tests pass.

When approved, write it verbatim into the artifact. Lock it.

### Step 5 — Decompose into 3+ sub-messages (hard gate)

> "Now split the takeaway into 3 (or 4) topic sentences that *support* it. Each is a self-contained sentence. If you remove one, the takeaway no longer stands."

Loop:

1. Speaker proposes 3.
2. Coach checks each: self-contained sentence? supports the takeaway? distinct from siblings? necessary?
3. If the speaker cannot produce 3 distinct ones, the takeaway is suspect. Push back: "Your takeaway may be too narrow or too vague. Revisit Step 4." Re-enter Step 4 if needed.

When approved, write all 3+ into the artifact.

### Step 6 — Narrative arc per sub-message

For each sub-message, ask the speaker to describe:

- **Tension build** — what does the audience not yet know that they need to?
- **Climax** — what's the moment of recognition?
- **Resolution** — what's the takeaway of *this arc* (which should connect back to one of the sub-messages)?

Stack the arcs so the resolution of one feeds the tension build of the next. Persist as an ordered list under "Narrative arcs."

### Step 7 — Visual strategy (only after content is locked)

For each sub-message, ask:

- What's the one image, diagram, or piece of code that makes this concrete?
- Is anything code? If so, what are the 2–4 lines that matter? (Reference `visuals-and-delivery.md`.)
- Is anything a diagram? If so, what's the reveal sequence?
- Is anything a demo? Live or pre-recorded? (Default: pre-recorded.)

Write each as a one-line entry in the artifact under "Visual plan."

### Step 8 — Delivery considerations (last)

Brief check-in only. No coaching on body/voice until rehearsal mode. Just confirm:

- Will the speaker be at a podium or on a stage? Mic type?
- Is the hook (first sentence) memorized?
- Any known unconscious movements to watch for (pacing, hand-fidgeting)?

Note in the artifact under "Delivery notes."

### Step 9 — Save

Write/update the artifact (see SKILL.md artifact schema). Confirm path with the speaker. Print the next recommended action (typically: "Schedule your first recorded rehearsal 2–3 weeks before the talk and switch to `rehearse` mode then.").

## Anti-patterns for the coach in `design` mode

- **Do not** discuss slides, fonts, or templates at any step. The speaker may try; refuse and cite Principle 1.
- **Do not** bundle multiple questions in one turn.
- **Do not** accept "everyone" as an audience subset.
- **Do not** accept a takeaway longer than 280 characters or one that fails any of the three tests.
- **Do not** move to Step 5 until Step 4 is locked.
- **Do not** invoke `visuals-and-delivery.md` before Step 7.
